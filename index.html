Automation recipes

Load a recipe, then Start. Open the tone’s Automation section to edit it. Loading replaces the bank.

Bass drop: sine, 160 → 40 Hz over 3 s. Sweep: 40 → 1000 Hz over 10 s. Sub test: 20 → 100 Hz over 15 s. Sub drop: 80 → 25 Hz over 4 s. These use exponential frequency ramps with Loop off. Wobble: 60 Hz sine with amplitude 0.08 → 0.65, sine curve, 0.5 s cycle, Loop on (2 Hz).

For tempo sync, cycle seconds = 60 ÷ BPM ÷ cycles per beat. At 120 BPM, use 0.5 s for quarter notes or 0.25 s for eighth notes. Add a pan lane for stereo motion or automate modulation depth for changing intensity.

Using BrowserToneGen
Enable a tone, choose its frequency and waveform, then Start. Add tones to mix frequencies. Solo plays only enabled solo voices; muted voices stay silent. Space starts/stops when focus is outside a control. Escape immediately silences the engine.
Levels & phase
Amplitude sets a voice's peak scale; dB is the equivalent amplitude ratio. Master dB changes the mix. Auto headroom divides by the summed amplitude budget and shows the compensation. The final 0.98 hard ceiling prevents sample overload and reports limiting. This is a digital test source; hardware volume and calibrated SPL are external.
Phase is an offset in degrees. Equal frequencies share a start clock; 180° reverses a sine. Both-channel center pan sends −3 dB to each channel; dedicated left/right routing sends full voice amplitude.
Sweeps, steps & sequences
Choose a tone's mode. Sweep moves between the frequency and end frequency; logarithmic spacing changes frequency exponentially with time. Ping-pong is an up/down round-trip. Steps divide that range into discrete frequencies. Transition is the fraction of each dwell used for a linear glide. Sequence uses the shared stage editor. Voice delays and duration apply to all modes.
Noise & harmonics
White is broadband random noise; pink emphasizes lower octaves, brown emphasizes bass, blue and violet emphasize high frequencies. Noise coloring is approximate. Additive combines signed harmonic coefficients; patterns generate odd/even or waveform approximations, then you can edit each coefficient.
Modulation & stereo
AM/tremolo change level. Ring multiplies by a sine LFO. FM changes frequency in Hz; vibrato depth is cents. Binaural pairs route nearby frequencies to separate channels; monaural pairs mix them. No therapeutic effects are claimed. Stereo utilities replace the current bank; related frequency groups append.
Automation & MIDI
Open Automation on a tone and add a lane. Choose a control, curve, From and To values, duration, delay, and optional loop. Ramps hold their final value; sine and triangle curves return to their starting value. Seconds are per complete cycle. Pause freezes automation. Stop and Start restart it. MIDI controls use CC mappings: Connect MIDI, select the input, target and control, Learn CC, move a knob, set the range and Add mapping. Automation overrides MIDI on its target. Use the Automation recipes panel for bass drops, sweeps, sub tests, sub drops and wobbles.
Export & storage
WAV renders from time zero using current voice and master settings. Mute exports silence. Normalization rescales the rendered peak to 0.95; it cannot undo limiting. Mono averages L/R, so opposite polarity can cancel. Long 96 kHz renders use substantial memory; use shorter renders on phones. Save/rename/duplicate/delete local presets or import/export versioned JSON. Share links contain the configuration and never autoplay.
Scope, spectrum and meters show generated digital output, not microphone response. Waveforms use anti-alias approximations; high-frequency modulation can introduce aliasing. A browser/soundcard cannot guarantee calibrated analog frequency, voltage, SPL or ultrasonic output.
Run locally through python -m http.server 8000. AudioWorklet needs HTTPS or localhost.


About
BrowserToneGen , a Circuit Drift Labs browser signal workbench.
Static ES modules, shared DSP, AudioWorklet playback and Worker WAV rendering. No accounts, uploads or telemetry. Presets and troubleshooting logs remain on your device.
Beta: device calibration, grey noise, speech announcements and offline PWA caching are outside this release. Chromium browser behavior is verified where reported in the repository audit; hardware listening and cross-browser testing are separate.
